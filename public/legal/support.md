# PackGo Support

Support provider: Huanwei Ye

For help with PackGo, contact us by email. This page has no contact form and does not collect submissions.

## Contact

Email: [airproductlab@gmail.com](mailto:airproductlab@gmail.com)

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, PackGo app version, and a description of the issue. Do not send a `.packcalm` backup, photos, trip details, checklist content, or other sensitive information unless we explicitly ask for the minimum information needed to investigate.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. After reinstalling PackGo or switching devices, use **Restore Purchases** on the PackGo Pro screen with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Refunds and billing disputes are handled by Apple.

## Privacy and Local Data

PackGo is local-first. You can export, import, or clear local data from **Settings > Local Data**. Read the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) for the current data-handling description.
