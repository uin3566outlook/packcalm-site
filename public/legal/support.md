# PackCalm Support

Support provider: yehuanwei

For help with PackCalm, contact us by email. This page has no contact form and does not collect submissions.

## Contact

Email: [airproductlab@gmail.com](mailto:airproductlab@gmail.com)

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, PackCalm app version, and a description of the issue. Do not send a `.packcalm` backup, photos, trip details, checklist content, or other sensitive information unless we explicitly ask for the minimum information needed to investigate.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. After reinstalling PackCalm or switching devices, use **Restore Purchases** on the PackCalm Pro screen with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Refunds and billing disputes are handled by Apple.

## Privacy and Local Data

PackCalm is local-first. You can export, import, or clear local data from **Settings > Local Data**. Read the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) for the current data-handling description.

# PackCalm 支持（简体中文）

支持提供方：yehuanwei

如需 PackCalm 使用帮助，请通过电子邮件联系我们。本页面不提供联系表单，也不会收集提交内容。

## 联系方式

邮箱：[airproductlab@gmail.com](mailto:airproductlab@gmail.com)

请注明你的 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、PackCalm 版本及问题描述。除非我们明确请求为排查问题所必需的最少信息，否则请勿发送 `.packcalm` 备份、照片、行程详情、清单内容或其他敏感信息。

## 购买

购买由 Apple 处理。重新安装 PackCalm 或更换设备后，请使用原购买 Apple 账号，在 PackCalm Pro 页面选择“恢复购买”。退款及账单争议由 Apple 处理。

## 隐私与本地数据

PackCalm 以本机存储为主。你可以在“设置 > 本地数据”中导出、导入或清除本地数据。当前数据处理说明请参阅[隐私政策](privacy-policy.html#zh-Hans)。
