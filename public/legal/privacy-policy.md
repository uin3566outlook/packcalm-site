# PackCalm Privacy Policy

Effective: September 3, 2026

Application provider: Huanwei Ye

PackCalm is a local-first packing checklist app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, when another service may receive it, and how you can delete it.

## 1. Data We Do Not Collect

The developer does not operate a PackCalm account system or server and does not receive your checklists, trip details, notes, photos, device identifiers, location, contacts, advertising data, analytics, or browsing activity. PackCalm does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Items, custom categories, reusable lists, trip names and dates, packing status, notes, app settings, onboarding choices, and processed custom icons are stored in PackCalm's private app container on your device. This information is used only to provide the features you request and is not transmitted to the developer.

## 3. Camera and Photos

If you choose to make a photo icon, PackCalm accesses only the photo you take or select. Image processing, subject extraction, cropping, and rendering happen on your device. PackCalm does not upload the image. The original photo is not retained by PackCalm; only the final processed icon is saved in the app container.

Camera access is requested only when you choose **Take Photo**. You can deny or revoke camera permission at any time in iOS Settings and continue using built-in icons or the system photo picker.

## 4. App Store Purchases

PackCalm uses Apple's StoreKit to offer and restore the one-time PackCalm Pro purchase. Apple processes payment and the App Store account. The app reads Apple-signed product and entitlement information to unlock Pro and stores a local entitlement flag. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of App Store data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. Export, Backup, and Sharing

PackCalm exports or shares information only when you start the action. A `.packcalm` backup may contain all local app data and processed photo icons and is not encrypted by PackCalm. A packing poster or other shared output may contain trip and checklist information. Once you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet or document picker, that destination provider's terms and privacy practices apply. The developer does not receive a copy.

## 6. Retention, Deletion, and Your Choices

Local data remains on your device until you delete individual content, choose **Settings > Local Data > Clear All Data**, or remove the app. Clearing the app does not delete backup or shared files you previously saved elsewhere; delete those from their destination separately. App Store purchase records are retained by Apple under Apple's policies and cannot be deleted by PackCalm.

Because the developer does not receive or maintain a copy of your local content, there is no developer-side account or content record to access, correct, or delete. You control camera permission in iOS Settings and control exported files wherever you chose to save them.

## 7. Public Legal Pages, Security, and Third Parties

PackCalm relies on the iOS app sandbox and Apple's platform security for local storage. No method of storage is risk-free, so protect your device and any exported files. PackCalm includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDK. Apple system services and any destination you deliberately choose for an export operate under their own privacy policies.

The app may load this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, or the Support page from the PackCalm website hosted with ChatGPT Sites so you can see the current version. The developer adds no cookies, advertising, analytics scripts, contact form, or account system to these pages. As with an ordinary web request, the hosting service may process standard technical information such as an IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, and request time to deliver and secure the site, as described in [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/). PackCalm does not send your trips, checklists, notes, photos, backups, purchase details, or other app content with the request. The developer does not receive the hosting logs, add tracking to these pages, or use connection data for advertising or user profiling. If the page cannot be reached, the app shows a bundled copy.

## 8. Support Communications

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any device or app details you voluntarily include. Your email provider and the developer's email provider process the message under their own terms and privacy policies. Support communications are used only to answer the request, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. They are retained only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and are not used for advertising or tracking. You may request deletion of a support conversation by emailing [airproductlab@gmail.com](mailto:airproductlab@gmail.com), subject to records that must be kept for security or legal reasons. Do not send a backup, photo, trip, or checklist content unless explicitly requested and necessary.

## 9. Children

PackCalm is a general-audience utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from any user through the app.

## 10. Changes and Contact

If PackCalm's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the new practice is introduced where required. The effective date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions or support requests, email [airproductlab@gmail.com](mailto:airproductlab@gmail.com) or visit the PackCalm Support page. Because PackCalm has no account system or developer server for app content, include only the information needed to describe your request and do not send a backup file or sensitive checklist content unless explicitly requested.
